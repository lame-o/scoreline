Scoreline is a free, native menu bar app for macOS. Pick your leagues and teams once: the scores sit in the menu bar, and a click opens the full live game, with a page built for each sport.

**MLB · NFL · College Football · NBA · WNBA · Premier League · Champions League · Europa League · LaLiga · Serie A · Bundesliga · Ligue 1 · Championship · MLS · FIFA World Cup · Counter-Strike 2 · World Climbing Series**

<br>

## Play theaters

Baseball, football and soccer plays come back to life in a small isometric theater the moment they happen: the swing and the throw, the snap and the run, the cross and the header. Batted balls fly on Statcast's own launch angle, exit velocity and distance, so a 410-foot home run lands where it really did. Basketball is next.

## Live game pages

Each game page is built for its sport, and it's live: charts redraw as plays land, and big moments (a home run, a touchdown, a goal) arrive with a small celebration you can turn down or off.

- **Baseball**: the count and runners, the at-bat pitch by pitch in the strike zone with MLB's pitch names, a spray chart of every ball in play, and the line score.
- **Football**: the drive on the field, down and distance, the red zone and the two-minute drill, scoring plays and win probability.
- **Basketball**: the shot chart, the game's flow and scoring runs, leaders, and the full box score.
- **Soccer**: the live pitch, the shot map, the match timeline, lineups and VAR decisions.

## Made for your teams

- **Follow your teams**: they lead the list, their schedule fills in when there's no game today, and their live score can sit in the menu bar.
- **Notifications** when your teams start, score and finish.
- **Where to Watch**: each game's official broadcasters and streams.
- **List or tiles**, a pinned scorebar for the game you're following, and sections you can reorder or hide.
- **Keyboard first**: arrows to move, Return to open, Esc to go back, ⌘R to refresh.
- **Accessible**: VoiceOver reads scores and statuses as words, Reduce Motion turns movement into fades, and shapes can stand in for color.

## Install

Paste this into Terminal:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lame-o/scoreline/main/install.sh | bash
```

It downloads the latest release into Applications and opens it ([read the script](install.sh) first if you like).

Or download `Scoreline.dmg` from [Releases](https://github.com/lame-o/scoreline/releases/latest), open it and drag Scoreline to Applications. Scoreline isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so the first time you open a downloaded copy macOS says it can't verify the developer: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.

After that, Scoreline keeps itself up to date (**Settings → Updates**).

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is Scoreline free?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. There's no account and no ads, and the source is MIT-licensed.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why does macOS say it can't verify the developer?</b></summary>
<br>
Scoreline is signed, but not with a paid Apple Developer ID, so Gatekeeper asks once. Open <b>System Settings → Privacy & Security</b> and click <b>Open Anyway</b>. The install one-liner above skips this step.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I watch games in Scoreline?</b></summary>
<br>
No. Scoreline is scores, stats and plays. Each game's <b>Where to Watch</b> lists its official broadcasters and streams.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is there an iPhone, iPad or Windows version?</b></summary>
<br>
The <a href="https://scoreline-web.lame-o.workers.dev">web app</a> runs in any modern browser and installs to your phone's home screen. The Mac app is macOS only.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which leagues are coming?</b></summary>
<br>
Requests are welcome: <a href="https://github.com/lame-o/scoreline/issues">open an issue</a> with the league you want and where its data comes from.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How current are the scores?</b></summary>
<br>
Scoreline reads the same public feeds the leagues' partners publish, and a live game's page refreshes every few seconds. Broadcasts and feeds run on their own delays, so a score can land a moment before or after you see it on TV.
</details>

## Privacy

No account, no analytics, no tracking. Scoreline only talks to the public sports data feeds it shows you, and your teams and settings stay on your Mac.

## Build from source

Scoreline builds with the Xcode Command Line Tools alone (no Xcode project, no packages):

```sh
xcode-select --install   # if you don't have the Command Line Tools
./build.sh --install     # builds Scoreline.app, copies it to /Applications and launches it
./test.sh                # the offline test suite (recorded API responses, no network)
```

`./build.sh --universal` builds for both Apple silicon and Intel. The web app lives in [`web/`](web) (React on Cloudflare Workers); [`web/ARCHITECTURE.md`](web/ARCHITECTURE.md) explains how to run it.

## Data and disclaimer

Scores and stats come from public feeds by ESPN, MLB (Statcast, via statsapi.mlb.com), bo3.gg and World Climbing (IFSC), among others. Scoreline is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, team, broadcaster or data provider. Team names and logos belong to their owners, and appear in the screenshots only as the app shows them. Those feeds aren't official APIs and can change without notice; if something breaks, please [open an issue](https://github.com/lame-o/scoreline/issues).
